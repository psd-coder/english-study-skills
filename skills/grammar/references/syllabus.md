# Grammar syllabus

The order below is a default. The learner's real errors decide what comes next; use the order when there is no clear error pattern, and the dependency chains always.

## Topics by level

Each line is one lesson. The tag after the dash links the topic to error data.

### A1
- to be, basic word order (SVO) - agreement
- subject pronouns and possessive adjectives - other
- present simple: positive, negative, questions with do/does - tense
- articles 1: a/an and basic the - article
- regular plurals; this, that, these, those - countability
- there is / there are - wordorder
- prepositions of place and time (in, on, at) - preposition
- can for ability and requests - modal
- present continuous for now - tense

### A2
- past simple, regular and common irregular verbs; did questions - tense
- past simple vs present simple - tense
- going to and present continuous for plans - tense
- countable and uncountable; some, any, much, many, a lot of - countability
- comparatives and superlatives - other
- adverbs of frequency and manner - wordorder
- object and possessive pronouns - other
- have to, must, should - modal

### B1
- present perfect for experience and unfinished time; for and since - tense
- present perfect vs past simple - tense
- past continuous with past simple - tense
- articles 2: the vs zero article; general statements - article
- future: will, going to, present continuous - tense
- first conditional; when/if + present - tense
- modals of possibility and deduction (may, might, could, must, can't) - modal
- relative clauses: who, which, that - wordorder
- gerunds and infinitives 1 (enjoy doing, want to do) - verbpattern
- quantifiers: few, little, enough, too - countability
- questions and embedded questions (where is it / where it is) - wordorder
- say and tell; make and do - collocation

### B2
- present perfect continuous; simple vs continuous - tense
- past perfect and sequencing past events - tense
- second conditional - tense
- third and mixed conditionals - tense
- the passive in common tenses - tense
- reported speech and backshift - tense
- non-defining and reduced relative clauses - wordorder
- gerunds and infinitives 2 (stop doing / stop to do) - verbpattern
- past modals: should have, must have, could have - modal
- articles 3: fixed phrases, place names, abstract nouns - article
- dependent prepositions (depend on, interested in) - preposition

### C1
- inversion (Hardly had I..., Not only...) - wordorder
- cleft sentences (It was... that..., What I need is...) - wordorder
- wish, if only, would rather - tense
- unreal forms: were I to..., I suggest he be... - tense
- discourse markers and cohesion - other
- hedging, emphasis and register - other
- nominalisation and formal structures - other
- used to, would, be used to, get used to - tense

## Dependency chains

Teach the earlier topic first. If the learner's error sits in an earlier topic, fix that one even when the error shows up in a later structure.

- to be and present simple come before everything.
- past simple -> present perfect -> present perfect continuous -> past perfect.
- present simple and continuous -> future forms.
- first -> second -> third -> mixed conditionals.
- active tenses -> passive.
- tense control -> reported speech.
- defining -> non-defining -> reduced relative clauses.

## From errors to topics

Read error categories from `study stats` and from recent samples (the `wrong`, `right` and `rule` fields say more than the category alone).

| What the learner does | Topic | Category |
|---|---|---|
| drops or misuses a, an, the | articles 1, 2 or 3, by level | article |
| past simple for recent or unfinished events ("I already did it") | present perfect vs past simple | tense |
| present for a state since the past ("I live here for 5 years") | present perfect (continuous) | tense |
| will after when or if ("when I will finish") | first conditional; time clauses | tense |
| "If I would know" or wrong conditional tenses | conditionals by level | tense |
| "He said he is tired" | reported speech | tense |
| "she go", "they was", "has/have" mixed | present simple agreement | agreement |
| "Where you work?", "Tell me where is it" | questions and embedded questions | wordorder |
| informations, advices, less errors | countable and uncountable; quantifiers | countability |
| "I enjoy to swim", "recommend to do", "look forward to meet" | gerunds and infinitives | verbpattern |
| "shouldn't" for "no need" ("you shouldn't waste time" for "you don't have to"), "must" for advice | have to, must, should | modal |
| "depend from", "arrive to" | dependent prepositions | preposition |
| say/tell, make/do mixed | say and tell; make and do | collocation |
| short, choppy sentences | relative clauses; linking words | wordorder |
| avoids or misforms the passive | the passive | tense |

When several patterns appear, choose by: most frequent, then the earliest in a dependency chain, then the one the learner cares about most.
