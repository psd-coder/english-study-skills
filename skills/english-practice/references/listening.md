# Listening

Real recordings with transcripts, from `shared/sources.md`. The learner listens on the source site; you use the transcript to write the tasks.

1. **Choose an episode** at the week's listening level from `plan.md` (or the learner's listening level, one step up for a stretch). Prefer a topic close to the learner's goal or life. Check `study episode-used <url>` and skip used ones.
2. **Fetch the transcript** with `curl -sL <url>` and read it. Pick the stretch to use: the whole episode for ELLLO, the whole 6 minutes or a 3-minute part for BBC, 3 to 5 minutes for TED.
3. **Before listening**: give the link, what to listen to (with times), and one or two gist questions to keep in mind. Pre-teach at most 3 words that would block understanding, and nothing else.
4. **First listen, for gist**: the learner listens once without the transcript and answers the gist questions.
5. **Second listen, for detail**: 4 to 6 questions: details, a number or name, what a speaker thinks or feels, and the meaning of one phrase in context. Ask the learner to count plays.
6. **Check**: for each wrong answer, quote the exact line from the transcript and say what made it hard (fast linking, a word they did not know, an idea said indirectly). This is where listening grows: hearing the gap between what they heard and what was said.
7. **Close the loop**: the learner reads the transcript once while listening a last time, and you name 2 to 4 chunks worth keeping.
8. Score: correct answers out of total, plus the number of plays, in the session log.

Optional output task when time allows: a 3-sentence summary of the episode, written or spoken (check it as a writing or speaking sample).
