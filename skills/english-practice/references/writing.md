# Writing

Real tasks with focused feedback and a rewrite. Correcting everything at once overwhelms the learner; focusing on the few patterns that repeat changes them.

1. **Task**: something the learner really writes or will write, sized to the time: a work message, an email, a short opinion, a summary of the week's listening, or an exam task if the goal is an exam. Give the reader, the purpose and a word count (60 to 120 words for B1, 120 to 200 for B2 and up). Ask them to write without a translator.
2. **Feedback** in this order:
   - The full corrected text, with every change marked, keeping the learner's meaning and voice. Fix errors only; style ideas come separately.
   - The top 2 or 3 patterns, chosen by how often they appear and how much they matter. For each: the rule in one sentence, the learner's examples, and the fixed forms. Prefer patterns that match the learner's frequent error categories.
   - One thing that works well, quoted.
   - One upgrade: a more natural chunk or linking word the learner can use next time.
3. **Rewrite**: a short new piece (3 to 5 sentences, on a related point) that uses the corrected patterns. This is part of the task. If the learner sends it in the same session, check it; if not, set it as open homework (see the SKILL.md).
4. **Log** the first version as a sample with all its errors, and the rewrite as a second sample.

Categories for errors are the ones in `../../shared/data-contract.md`. Typing slips (a doubled letter, a wrong key) are not errors; leave them out of the list and the sample.
