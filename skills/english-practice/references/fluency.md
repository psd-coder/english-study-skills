# Fluency

Fluency work uses easy, known material and pushes speed. No new language: the learner should know everything in the task. The aim is to make known language come out faster and more automatically.

## 4-3-2 speaking

The learner talks about one familiar topic three times: 4 minutes, then 3, then 2 (shorten to 2-1.5-1 for A2-B1 or short sessions). Each time they cover the same content in less time. Only the last version goes through Handy. Feedback: what got faster, which chunks came out naturally, and at most one correction.

## Timed retell

The learner retells a listening episode or a text from this week, in 60 to 90 seconds, spoken (Handy) or typed against a timer. Count words per minute and compare with the last retell in the session log.

## Speed reading

A text clearly below the learner's level (one level lower, about 98% known words), 300 to 500 words. The learner notes the start and end time, then answers 3 quick gist questions. Track words per minute across weeks; the goal is faster reading with the same understanding.

## Shadowing

As described in `speaking.md`, with lines from material the learner already knows well.

Log fluency sessions with the measured speed (`"wpm": <n>`) so the trend shows in the session log.
