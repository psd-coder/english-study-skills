# Reading

Real texts at the right level: the learner should know about 95% of the words for practice (stretch reading) and about 98% for fluency reading.

1. **Choose a text** at the week's level that serves the goal: a learner news article (VOA Learning English, BBC Learning English), a blog post or documentation page from the learner's field, or the transcript of a recent listening episode. Check `study episode-used <url>` and log it after use. When no suitable real text is reachable, write an original text at the level and say it was written for the lesson.
2. **Before reading**: one line of context and one purpose question ("What problem does the author want to solve?").
3. **Questions** (4 to 6): the main idea, two details, one inference (what the author means but does not say), and one word or phrase in context.
4. **Check** each answer, quoting the line that holds the answer.
5. **Language from the text**: 2 to 4 chunks for the vocab store, and one grammar pattern from the text if it matches the week's grammar focus.
6. Optional output: the learner gives their own opinion on the text in 3 to 5 sentences (a writing sample).

Score the answers in the session log.
